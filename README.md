# 📚 EduLoop

### Pass the Book, Not the Cost ✦

EduLoop is an AI-powered student-to-student academic book exchange platform that helps students buy and sell used academic books at affordable prices.

Built for **BharatAgentic 2026**.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Academic textbooks can be expensive, while many students already have books they no longer need.

Students often struggle to:

- Find affordable second-hand academic books
- Find books relevant to their course and subject
- Decide a fair resale price
- Match with students who need their books

---

## 💡 Solution

EduLoop creates an intelligent student book marketplace where students can:

📤 List books they want to sell  
🔍 Describe what book they need in natural language  
🤖 Use AI to search and match available books  
💰 Get AI-assisted resale price recommendations  
📩 Request books from other students  

---
